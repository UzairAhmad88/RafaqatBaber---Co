The new Rafaqat Babar & Co. website will be a premium digital portfolio for an accounting 
and financial advisory firm. 
The website should communicate four things immediately: 
1. Trust 
2. Professional expertise 
3. Financial precision 
4. Modern business thinking

• Structured 
• Trustworthy 
• Smooth

• Fast 
The design should avoid unnecessary complexity. 
The website should feel closer to a premium financial advisory/consulting firm's digital 
profile than a traditional accounting firm's old-style website.
